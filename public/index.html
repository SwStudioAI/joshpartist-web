About

Queens, New York.

Josh.P is an electronic music artist and producer based in Queens, New York. His sound moves through Deep House, Progressive House and Melodic Techno, always carrying his Latin roots and an emotional, cinematic touch — piano and guitar lines that sing over the beat.

He is the founder of SwMusic, his own music platform and creative ecosystem, and the creator of Electro Hip Metal — a fusion of industrial techno, hip-hop rap and gospel metal introduced with “No Kings in the Dark.”

For Josh.P, music is never just rhythm: it's a message.
