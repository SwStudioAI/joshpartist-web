About

Queens, New York.

Josh.P is an electronic music artist and producer based in Queens, New York, and the founder of SwMusic — an independent platform spanning streaming, radio, and immersive musical experiences. He creates without compromise, blending deep house, progressive house, melodic techno, and Latin fusion into something deeply personal: emotive piano lines, rolling basslines, and synths that lift somewhere higher.

Through SwMusic he also builds the technology behind the music — a platform with Android, iOS and web apps. And with “No Kings in the Dark” he introduced Electro Hip Metal, a genre of his own creation.

“Run Into The Sun” debuted at #70 on the Hypeddit Top 100 Progressive House chart and climbed into the Top 30.

For Josh.P, music is not background noise. It is a conversation without words.
