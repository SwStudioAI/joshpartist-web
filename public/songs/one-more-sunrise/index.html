One More Sunrise

One more sunrise, one more day — a single dawn traveling the world, from eighties Jamaica to today's New York.

The story

«One More Sunrise» is built on a simple, stubborn hope: no matter how dark the night was, the sun comes up again. Musically it fuses reggae warmth with a tech-house drive — one sunrise traveling around the planet: Jamaica in the eighties and nineties, Africa, Ecuador in 2010, and today's New York.

The official music video premiered on YouTube on November 9, 2026. On the Hypeddit Top 100 House chart the single climbed to #58 on November 11 and to #48 the next day.

Its Spotify Canvas shows a figure walking through a crowd and transforming — sun, young man, woman, elder, dove — the same sunrise lived by every generation.
