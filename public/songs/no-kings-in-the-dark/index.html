The story

«No Kings in the Dark» is the first track of ELECTRO HIP METAL, a genre created by Josh.P in October 2026: industrial techno fused with hip-hop rap and gospel metal.

Its story is a parable: in the darkness there is no king, only a prison. The one who escapes does not stay free — he returns, fire in his chest turned into a sword, to liberate the others who are still inside.

The concept is being developed as a cinematic, video-game-style music video: seven characters, biomechanical alien jailers, and a chain of scenes that moves from the cell to the light.
